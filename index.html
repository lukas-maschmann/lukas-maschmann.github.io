I am a Ph.D. candidate at the Department of Economics at Lund University. My supervisors are Roel van Veldhuizen and Pol Campos-Mercade. I completed my M.Sc. in Economics at Lund University in 2022. In summer 2026, I was visiting Dorothea Kübler at WZB in Berlin.

My research focuses on how individuals process information and make economic decisions. I am particularly interested in how the presentation and interpretation of information shape choices in different settings, including education and financial markets.

I am on the 2026/27 job market.

Research Interests

Behavioral Economics Experimental Economics Labor Economics Education Information & Decision-Making
